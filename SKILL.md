## Panel-specific overlay

Place an irregular hand-painted rectangular field inside wide paper margins. Organise most of the simplified hand-drawn subject within it and let a few meaningful contours cross its brushed, broken edges. Keep a small focus, vast whitespace and 2–4 fresh source-derived colours. Avoid full-bleed fields, hard template borders and literal scene copying.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
